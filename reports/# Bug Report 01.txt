# Bug Report 01

Bug Title: Template creation fails with “Token not authorized” error.

Bug ID: SERRI-001  
Module: Templates  
Environment: Serri CRM Beta  
Browser: Chromium  

Precondition: User is logged into the application.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open the Serri CRM application.
2. Navigate to the Templates module.
3. Click Create Template.
4. Enter the required template details.
5. Submit the template.

Expected Result:  
The template should be created successfully and appear in the Templates list.

Actual Result:  
The application displays the error message: `Token not authorized`. The template is not created.

Reproducibility: Reproduced twice.

Severity: Medium (provisional)  
Priority: High (provisional)

Status: Open – Requires investigation.

Note: This may be related to BSP authorization or integration configuration. Further investigation is required to confirm the root cause.
